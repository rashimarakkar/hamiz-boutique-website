My story

I am Hameeda Rashid.

From my school and college days, I carried a special love for fashion. I was drawn to colours, fabrics, silhouettes and the joy of creating something beautiful.

My academic path led me to a degree in Commerce, followed by professional experience as an accountant. It was a meaningful part of my journey, teaching me discipline, care and attention to detail.

But my interest in fashion never faded. It remained a dream I kept close. Over time, I realised that the happiness I felt imagining and creating designs was something I wanted to make part of my everyday life.

Today, I am grateful to have chosen my passion as my profession. Through Hamiz Boutique, I get to create, customise and create outfits for meaningful moments. I love the feeling of helping someone wear something personal, meaningful and beautiful.

With love, Hameeda
